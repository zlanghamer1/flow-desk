They are the same for every visitor. They do not consider your finances, your goals, or how much risk you can take.

The operator is not your adviser

[Operator legal name] is not a registered investment adviser, broker-dealer, or financial planner. The operator does not manage money, hold accounts, or take orders. If you want advice for your situation, talk to a licensed professional.

Options carry real risk

Options are not suitable for everyone. Some strategies can lose more than the money you put in. Before you trade options, read the Options Clearing Corporation's booklet Characteristics and Risks of Standardized Options. Your broker provides it, and it is available at theocc.com.

The data is delayed and estimated

Stock prices are about 15 minutes behind. Options data is 15-minute-delayed Cboe data. Figures such as net flow, flow percent, aggressor tilt, unusual-activity tags, and the biggest-orders board are estimates built from delayed, sampled, aggregated data. Free data cannot see who bought and who sold. A number on this site is not a fact about what any trader did.

No performance claim

The scores, verdicts, and auto-drawn chart lines on this site have not been shown to predict returns. Past patterns do not predict future results.
