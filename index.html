The individual contracts with the most money traded through them today, ranked across every name on the watch list. Strikes within 20% of the stock price, expiring inside 6 months. Free data publishes daily totals per contract, not a trade-by-trade tape — so paid services showing "a $6M sweep hit the tape" are seeing something this cannot.
